# ADR 0008: Staged delivery and self-hosting

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

The product has a broad 1.0 scope and needs continuous development without losing agreed requirements.

## Decision

Deliver seven usable vertical stages governed by the committed specification, roadmap, coverage matrix, decision records, tests, and stage completion evidence. Once the foundational harness is usable, Tasktra manages its own continuing work. Stage progression continues autonomously except for new product decisions, authority expansion, destructive action, or unresolved tradeoffs.

## Consequences

The repository—not chat memory—is the durable plan. Each stage must identify evidence and limitations before the next stage begins.
